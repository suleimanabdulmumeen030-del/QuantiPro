    About us

    QuantiPro was built to make takeoff and BOQ work faster and easier to check, for students learning measurement and for professionals working to a deadline.

    We follow the way a quantity surveyor works. Dimensions go in once, quantities come out with their workings, and nothing is hidden behind a total. You can change any figure and see the effect straight away.

    QuantiPro is a tool for your judgement, not a replacement for it. Always check quantities against your drawings and your adopted method of measurement before you price or submit.
